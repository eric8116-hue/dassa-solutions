A failing PBX should not mean rewiring every room.

When the old PBX in the back closet starts dropping calls or losing its link to your PMS, the usual answer is rip and replace: new network cable to every room and a new IP phone on every nightstand.

That means a large equipment bill, open walls, and rooms out of service while the work happens. You have another option.

Modern where you need it. Unchanged where you do not.

A modern front desk

Give the front desk, reservations, and back office HD IP phones. Route busy call traffic, queue reservation calls, and keep outside calls from stacking up during the morning check-out rush.

Guests keep the phones they know

We install a gateway that connects the new cloud system to the copper wiring already in your walls. Guest rooms keep their current analog phones, and you skip the cost and disruption of a property-wide hardware swap.

Your PMS and phones stay in sync

The system connects to your existing property management system. Depending on your PMS, that can mean automated wake-up calls, room status updates such as Maid in Room or Room Clean, and billing posted without front-desk keystrokes.

PMS features vary by system. We check yours before recommending anything.
