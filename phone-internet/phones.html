The right phone for each spot in your business.

Color desk phone

See every line at a glance and send a call to another department with one button.

  • Color screen with programmable speed-dial keys
  • Dedicated Hold, Transfer, Conference, and Speaker buttons
  • Two Gigabit Ethernet ports, so it shares one wall jack with a computer
  • Powered through the network cable (PoE), so no power adapter
  • Headset ports (RJ9 and USB)

Best for

The front desk, accounting, and service advisors. See which lines are busy, transfer without hunting for a menu, and keep the audio clear on a busy morning.

Rugged cordless handset

Answer at the lift, not back at the desk.

  • Range up to 150+ ft indoors and 900+ ft outdoors in open line of sight
  • Built for drops, dust, and grease
  • 18+ hours of talk time and 200+ hours of standby per charge
  • Speakerphone, belt clip, and vibrate alert for loud areas

Best for

Technicians, bay managers, parts runners, and warehouse staff. Walk from the counter to the back lot and keep the call, so an urgent dispatch does not wait for someone to reach a desk.

Conference speakerphone

Everyone at the table is heard.

  • Microphones pick up voices in a 360° circle, up to 20 ft
  • Background noise reduction for air conditioning, shop noise, and street traffic
  • Touch controls with a visible mute light

Best for

Client consultations, management reviews, and team meetings. Talk normally. Nobody has to lean toward the phone or raise their voice.

Four ways calls reach the right person.

Every caller hears a greeting first

Callers get a professional greeting even when your staff is on another line. They press 1 for Service, 2 for Sales, or 0 for the Front Desk. Set different greetings for business hours, lunch, holidays, and after hours, with emergency calls forwarded to whoever you choose.

Calls keep moving until someone answers

Ring several phones at once, or one after another. If the front counter is busy, the call moves to the back office or a designated cell phone before the customer gives up.

Reach anyone with three digits

Put every location, remote estimator, and back-office employee on one dial plan. Dial an extension, park a call for pickup in another bay, or transfer a caller in two button presses.

Voicemail lands in your inbox

Each message is recorded, transcribed, and emailed to you with the audio attached. Read what the customer needs on your phone between jobs, and call the urgent ones back first.
